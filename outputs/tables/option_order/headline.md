Two things to read off this. Reordering does not remove affiliated selection: the rate stays in the 20-50% range wherever the affiliated option sits, and the pooled First-Last contrast is not significant. Omitting the option does remove it, dropping every model to under 2% - so the prompt's option list is what makes the choice visible, not what drives it.
